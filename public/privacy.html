The Vaschuk Family: privacy

This is a private site for one family. Only Google accounts the family has invited can sign in.

What the site keeps

The family chat, the family calendar, saved places, and what members asked the family assistant to remember. Each member's private conversation with the assistant, and anything it remembers just for them, is visible only to that member. Everything is stored in Google Firebase and used only to run this site.

Google Calendar

A member can choose to connect their Google Calendar. The site then asks Google for permission to create its own "Vaschuk Family" calendar in that account and manage the events on it (calendar.app.created), plus the account's email address to show which account is connected. It cannot see or change any other calendar or event in that Google account.

The site uses this access only to copy the family calendar into that one calendar and keep it up to date. Google user data is not sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The access token is stored where only the site's server code can read it.

Disconnecting (Assistant → Just me → Disconnect) deletes the "Vaschuk Family" calendar from the Google account, revokes the site's access, and deletes the stored token. Access can also be removed at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections.

The assistant

Requests to the family assistant are answered by Claude (Anthropic), which receives the request together with the family calendar, places and memory needed to answer it.

Questions or deletion

Ask the family member who invited you; they can remove your account and its data.
